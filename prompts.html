Onboarding Welcome Email
"Write a warm, professional welcome email for a new client who just signed. Include: what happens next, timeline, what I need from them, and how to reach me. Business: [type]. Service: [service]. Tone: confident and reassuring."
Email
Overdue Invoice Reminder
"Write a firm but professional email chasing an overdue invoice. Invoice: [amount], due: [date], now [X] days overdue. Tone: direct without being aggressive. Include payment details placeholder. Keep under 100 words."

Declining a Project Politely
